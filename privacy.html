Privacy Policy – Clip2fou Poster

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Data we access

With the account owner's permission (TikTok Login Kit), the app accesses: basic profile information (display name, avatar), the ability to upload and publish videos, and the list and statistics of the account's own videos.

How we use it

This data is used only to show who is posting, to publish videos chosen by the account owner, and to show the owner how their own videos perform.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and video statistics are stored privately for the account owner only. No data is sold or shared with third parties, and no data about other TikTok users is collected.

Deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Apps), after which stored tokens are deleted.
